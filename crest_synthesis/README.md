## Decision

The cross-contract `J` and `O` results are owned by a distinct **CREST synthesis
unit**.

They do not belong to any one companion repository because their quantified
statements essentially combine contracts from at least two of:

- CCOC future sufficiency;
- MLTR inherited-semantic coherence;
- MRM mechanism robustness; and
- CED evidential licensing.

MRM remains the mechanism-robust macro-law repository. It is only the current
physical host for the synthesis unit and philosophy manuscript.

## Routing rule

Route work by its central quantified object.

| Central object | Owner |
|---|---|
| independently optimized closed/open future interface | CCOC |
| one inherited source law and source-relative target repair | MLTR |
| retained candidate mechanisms and candidate-safe prediction | MRM |
| finite/noisy evidence, failure architecture, calibration, reporting risk | CED |
| essential coupling of two or more companion contracts | CREST synthesis unit |

A cross-contract candidate enters the synthesis unit only if it proves a new
noncommutation, impossibility, necessary-and-sufficient boundary, or coupled
minimality statement. Applying a generic algorithm after relabelling variables is
not enough.

## Development stop

After J1–J7 and O1, no new synthesis theorem family should be added under the
`mrm/` package path.

Allowed before physical extraction:

- proof correction;
- complexity and prior-art audit;
- regression tests;
- claim-firewall repair;
- migration work; and
- manuscript consolidation.

Not allowed before extraction:

- J8 or a new O-family;
- generic stochastic, partial-observation, simulation, or repair extensions without
  a new coupled theorem;
- presenting CREST results as part of MRM's publication core.

Their decision versions are NP-complete by direct weighted-set-cover reductions.
Exactness must not be read as polynomial tractability. See
`docs/crest_repair_complexity_boundary_2026-08-18.md`.
