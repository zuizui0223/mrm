## Status vocabulary

- **FORMAL** — directly supported by an indexed analytic theorem under stated finite premises.
- **INTERPRETIVE** — ecological/philosophical reading that follows from the formal result but is not itself a theorem.
- **OPEN** — live metatheoretical or novelty hypothesis; may be argued, but must not be written as established fact.
- **REJECT** — wording known to exceed the mathematics or rediscover established prior art.

## 1. Program-level thesis

### C1

**Sentence:**

> The adequacy of a usable coarse ecological state equivalence is relative to the scientific contract under which it is used.

**Status:** **INTERPRETIVE / program thesis.**

**Basis:** CCOC, MLTR, MRM, and CED each provide a different formal way in which one proposed state equivalence can fail under a changed future, inherited semantics, retained response family, or evidence contract.

**Boundary:** This does not prove that every possible ecological notion of state adequacy is contract-relative in exactly these four ways.

### C2

**Sentence:**

> Ecological state identity is contract-relative, not intrinsic.

**Status:** **OPEN / rhetorical shorthand only.**

**Use rule:** Allowed only if immediately narrowed to **scientific coarse-state representation adequacy**.

**Reason:** Ecology/philosophy already contains substantive theories of ecological-system identity. CREST does not prove a complete metaphysics of identity.

### C3

**Sentence:**

> Contract-relative does not mean arbitrary.

**Status:** **INTERPRETIVE.**

**Basis:** Once a contract is declared, proposed merges can be objectively rejected by legal future separators, failed inherited factorization, response-type disagreement, or evidence classes containing multiple target values.

**Boundary:** The contracts themselves are scientific modelling commitments; CREST does not prove they are uniquely correct for a real ecosystem.

## 2. Future sufficiency — CCOC

**Status:** **INTERPRETIVE.**

**Status:** **INTERPRETIVE, not novel by itself.**

**CREST-safe use:** Cite CCOC only for the specific cross-grammar lower bound and sharpness claim.

### C7

**Sentence:**

**Status:** **REJECT.**

**Reason:** CCOC supplies finite positive portability/blanket criteria as well as lower-bound counterexamples. It does not prove universal nonexistence.

### C8

**Sentence:**

> CCOC introduces the minimal future-predictive state.

**Status:** **REJECT.**

**Reason:** generic minimal future-/behavior-preserving state representations are established in automata theory, computational mechanics, bisimulation, MDP abstraction, and predictive-state work.

**Boundary:** Not a general theorem that all ecological path dependence reduces to this condition.
