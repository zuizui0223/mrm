Use citations to do one of three jobs only:

1. **acknowledge direct prior ownership** of a neighboring idea;
2. **support a boundary statement** explaining what CREST is not claiming;
3. **locate the philosophical conversation** in which the four-contract synthesis is being offered.

**Collier, J., & Cumming, G. S. (2011).** A Dynamical Approach to Ecosystem Identity. In *Philosophy of Ecology*, Handbook of the Philosophy of Science, Vol. 11, pp. 201–218. Elsevier. DOI: `10.1016/B978-0-444-51673-2.50008-X`.

**Delettre, O. (2021).** Identity of ecological systems and the meaning of resilience. *Journal of Ecology*, **109**, 3147–3156. DOI: `10.1111/1365-2745.13655`.

Use for:

- direct ecological-identity philosophy;
- multiple meanings of ecological identity;
- warning against claiming that CREST is the first multi-axis ecological-identity framework.
