# CREST philosophy citation-to-claim audit — 2026-08-17

> **Status:** final citation-placement audit for the current minimal philosophy manuscript. This document asks whether each in-text citation actually supports the boundary statement to which it is attached. It is not a novelty audit and does not infer priority from absence of search results.

## Audit rule

A citation passes only if it performs at least one necessary job:

1. establishes direct prior ownership of an idea that CREST must not claim as new;
2. supports the exact neighboring framework named in the sentence;
3. supports a philosophical boundary that changes how the CREST claim must be worded.

A citation fails if it is merely topically related or decoratively broad.

## Stop rule

The minimal philosophy draft now has enough literature to support its claim boundaries. Further citation expansion should occur only in response to one of three triggers:

1. a reviewer or specialist identifies a missing direct predecessor;
2. a manuscript sentence is strengthened and therefore needs a stronger prior-art boundary;
3. journal positioning requires a more explicit conversation with one philosophical literature.

Absent one of those triggers, do not expand the reference list merely to appear comprehensive.
