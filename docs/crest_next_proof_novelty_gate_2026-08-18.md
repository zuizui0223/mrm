# CREST next-proof novelty gate — 2026-08-18

> **Status:** active stop/routing decision after J1–J7. This document does not
> introduce a theorem. It applies the CREST rule that a new synthesis result must
> expose a genuinely coupled noncommutation, impossibility, or minimality statement
> rather than rename established viability, repair, abstraction, or inference
> machinery.

## 1. Gate criteria

A candidate may become a new CREST theorem only when all of the following hold.

1. **Coupling:** at least two existing CREST contracts enter the quantified
   statement essentially.
2. **New quantifier structure:** the claim is not one companion theorem after a
   variable rename.
3. **Nontrivial output:** the result gives a sharp obstruction, necessary-and-
   sufficient boundary, or coupled minimum—not merely a generic algorithm applied
   to ecological labels.
4. **Prior-art safety:** generic fixed points, safety games, partial observation,
   diagnosability, model repair, simulation, or Bayesian design are treated as
   substrate.
5. **Manuscript value:** the result shortens or sharpens the CREST story rather than
   adding another parallel branch.

## 2. Candidate decisions

| Candidate | Gate decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| partial-observation / finite-memory controlled lift | **HOLD** | partial-observation safety games and finite-memory strategy theory are mature; no CREST-specific coupling theorem is yet isolated |
| stochastic or chance-constrained viability/repair | **HOLD** | stochastic reachability, viability, and risk constraints are broad established fields; the current candidate is an extension domain, not a sharp synthesis claim |
| weakest possible J5 morphism | **STOP as headline** | weakest simulation/abstraction conditions are primarily generic representation theory unless a new evidence or repair obstruction is proved |
| arbitrary richer J4/J7 repair language | **STOP as headline** | adding redirection, relabelling, or more edit types is generic model repair unless the edit types provably fail to commute with another CREST gate |
| empirical inference of synchronizations, action roles, or costs | **ROUTE TO APPLICATION** | this is an estimation/validation programme rather than a finite theorem implied by the current contracts |
| minimum repair constrained by downstream J1/CED licensing | **SUPPORTING OBSTRUCTION ONLY** | it genuinely couples repair and evidence, but the generic constrained minimum is standard; retain only the strict noncommutation witness unless a stronger general boundary emerges |

## 3. Accepted result: CREST-O1

The only current candidate that passes the coupling test without opening a broad
new field is the repair/evidence ordering obstruction:

\[
\boxed{
R_{\mathrm{struct}}^*
<
R_{\mathrm{licensed}}^*
}
\]

can occur.

`docs/crest_repair_evidence_noncommutation_2026-08-18.md` proves an explicit J7×J1/CED
witness in which:

- the unique cheapest controlled-carrier repair costs `1`;
- its downstream joint state is not resolved by the evidence;
- a cost-`2` repair yields the least fully licensed state; and
- the target remains reportable even under the cheaper unlicensed full state.

This is retained as **CREST-O1**, a synthesis obstruction. It is not promoted to
`J8` because the finite constrained optimization surrounding the witness is not a
new general mathematical object.

## 4. Development decision

After O1, theorem expansion stops until one of two things happens:

1. a sharper general statement is found—for example, a necessary-and-sufficient
   condition under which J7 optimization and J1/CED licensing commute; or
2. a real ecological application exposes a coupled failure not expressible by the
   current J1–J7 ladder.

The active main line is therefore **consolidation**, not theorem count:

```text
J3/J6 carrier
  -> J4/J7 typed repair
  -> J1 state + evidence gate
  -> J2/J5 comparison
  -> O1 warning: cheapest carrier repair may not be cheapest licensed repair
```

The J/O series is owned by the separate CREST synthesis unit described in
`crest_synthesis/README.md`; it is not part of the MRM mechanism theorem family.

## 5. Manuscript routing

O1 may enter the CREST philosophy manuscript only as one short diagnostic example:

> structural feasibility, representational adequacy, and evidential licensing are
> distinct optimization targets; satisfying the cheapest upstream target does not
> guarantee the downstream one.

Do not add a new theorem section, a new acronym family, or a claim that CREST first
identified observability-aware repair. The formal theorem ladder remains J1–J7;
O1 explains why the gates must remain visible.
